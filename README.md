# Fake Internship and Job-Offer Scam Detector

## Problem Statement
Engineering students are targeted by fake internships, fraudulent job offers, fee-based offer letters, and task scams through Telegram, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn.

## Objective
To help students identify suspicious internship and job offers before sharing personal information or making payments.

## Target Users
- Pre-final and final-year engineering students
- Students searching for off-campus internships and jobs
- College placement cells

## Proposed Solution
The proposed system aims to identify potential scam indicators such as requests for registration fees, unverified recruiters, unrealistic job promises, and requests for sensitive personal information.

## Expected Outcome
A student-friendly tool that helps students recognize recruitment scam warning signs and make safer decisions.

## Project Status
Problem statement and proposed solution. Implementation is in progress.

## Disclaimer
This tool provides preliminary warnings and cannot guarantee that a job offer is genuine or fraudulent.
